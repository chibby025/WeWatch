## 💰 Pricing

### CPM Model (Cost Per Mille)
- **$2.00 per 1,000 impressions**
- Pay only for views, not clicks
- No hidden fees

### Minimum Budget
- **$50 per campaign**
- Campaigns auto-pause when budget is reached
- Add more budget anytime to keep ads running

## 🎨 Ad Formats

### 1. Banner Ads
**Display**: Between posts in Discover feed  
**Format**: Image (JPG, PNG, GIF)  
**Size**: Up to 5MB  
**Aspect Ratio**: 3:4 (portrait)  
**Best For**: Brand awareness, promotions, app downloads

### 2. Video Pre-roll Ads
**Display**: Before video content in watch sessions  
**Format**: Video (MP4, WebM)  
**Size**: Up to 50MB  
**Duration**: 5-second forced view, then skippable  
**Best For**: Product demos, trailers, storytelling

### 3. Sponsored Rooms (Coming Soon)
**Display**: Featured section in Discover tab  
**Format**: Custom room card  
**Best For**: Events, live shows, interactive experiences

## 🎯 Targeting Options

### Age Targeting
- Set age range from 13 to 99 years
- Example: 18-34 for young adults
- Precise targeting based on user profiles

### Content Rating
Match your ad to appropriate content:
- **General**: All ages, family-friendly
- **PG**: Parental guidance suggested
- **PG-13**: 13+ recommended
- **R**: 17+ restricted
- **Mature**: 18+ adult content

### Geographic (Coming Soon)

## 📊 Analytics Dashboard

Track your campaign performance in real-time:

### Key Metrics
- **Impressions**: Total views of your ad
- **Clicks**: Users who clicked your ad
- **CTR**: Click-through rate (clicks ÷ impressions × 100)
- **Spent**: Total amount used from budget
- **Remaining**: Budget still available

### Performance Insights
- View trends over time
- See peak engagement hours
- Optimize based on data

## 🚀 How to Create Your First Campaign

### Step 1: Campaign Details
1. Choose a campaign name
2. Select ad type (banner or video pre-roll)
3. Set start and end dates

### Step 2: Upload Creative
1. Click "Choose File" or drag & drop
2. Preview your ad before uploading
3. Enter the URL users will visit when they click

**Tips**:
- Use high-quality images (1080px recommended)
- Keep videos under 30 seconds for best results
- Ensure your landing page is mobile-friendly

### Step 3: Targeting
1. Set age range (default: 18-65)
2. Choose content rating (default: All)
3. Select countries (optional)

**Tips**:
- Narrow targeting increases relevance
- Broad targeting increases reach
- Test different audiences

### Step 4: Budget & Review
1. Enter your budget (minimum $50)
2. Review estimated performance
3. Preview your campaign settings
4. Submit for approval

**Estimated Performance**:
- Budget: $100 → 50,000 impressions
- Expected CTR: 1-3%
- Expected clicks: 500-1,500

## ✅ Campaign Approval Process

### Timeline
- **Submission**: Instant
- **Review**: 1-24 hours
- **Go Live**: Immediately after approval

### Approval Criteria
✅ Ad content is appropriate  
✅ No prohibited content (hate speech, violence, etc.)  
✅ Landing page is safe and functional  
✅ Budget is sufficient ($50 minimum)  
✅ Targeting is valid

### If Rejected
- You'll receive an email with the reason
- Fix the issue and resubmit
- Common issues: Low-quality creative, broken links, inappropriate content

## 🛠️ Best Practices

### Creative Design
- **Use bold colors** to stand out
- **Include a clear call-to-action** (e.g., "Shop Now", "Learn More")
- **Keep text minimal** and easy to read
- **Test multiple creatives** to see what works

### Targeting Strategy
- **Start broad**, then narrow based on performance
- **Match content rating** to your brand (family ads on general content)
- **Test age ranges** to find your sweet spot

### Budget Management
- **Start small** ($50-100) to test
- **Scale up** successful campaigns
- **Set reminders** to check performance weekly
- **Add budget** before campaigns pause
