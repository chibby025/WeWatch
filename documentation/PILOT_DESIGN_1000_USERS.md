LETSWATCHOUT PILOT DESIGN: 1000 USERS IN 90 DAYS

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Deploy immersive 3D co-learning platform across 5 Nigerian universities, reaching 1000+ students in 90 days while serving marginalized groups: 200 remote/rural learners, 50 students with disabilities, 300 low-income students.

PRIMARY OBJECTIVES
• Acquire 1,000+ users across 5 universities with 60%+ weekly retention
• Generate 500+ virtual study sessions; achieve 15% paid conversion rate
• Enable equitable access for 550+ marginalized students (remote, disabled, low-income)
• Establish institutional partnerships and 50-member student ambassador network

PILOT TIMELINE & STRUCTURE (90 Days)

Phase 1: Foundation (Days 1-30) – 200 Users
• Formalize MoUs with universities; recruit 50 student ambassadors
• Launch campus hubs (computer labs with high-speed internet)
• Host demo events; onboard 5 lecturer champions per university

Phase 2: Expansion (Days 31-60) – 600 Users
• Campus-wide rollout via student clubs and referral program
• Launch paid sessions (₦500-1,000) and creator program for tutors
• Deploy accessibility features (screen readers, keyboard navigation)

Phase 3: Scale (Days 61-90) – 1,000+ Users
• Target 100+ paid sessions/week; enable peer tutoring marketplace
• Conduct impact surveys; present data to administrations for adoption
• Plan expansion to 10 additional universities

TARGET USER GROUPS (Marginalization Focus)

1. Remote/Rural Students (200 users, 20%)
Challenge: 50+ km from campus, miss classes due to transport costs
Solution: Virtual classrooms eliminate commute, provide 24/7 access
Partnership: NYSC/Community Development Committees for recruitment

2. Students with Mobility Disabilities (50 users, 5%)
Challenge: Wheelchair users, chronic illness face campus navigation barriers
Solution: Fully accessible 3D environments with screen reader support
Partnership: Joint National Association of Persons with Disabilities (JONAPWD) for recruitment and accessibility validation

3. Low-Income Students (300 users, 30%)
Challenge: Cannot afford campus housing, limited study group access
Solution: Save ₦20,000+/month on transport/housing costs
Partnership: Student Financial Aid offices and scholarship programs

4. General Student Population (450 users, 45%)
Value: Enhanced study experiences, immersive watch parties, social connection

PARTNERSHIP STRUCTURE & RESPONSIBILITIES

Tier 1: University Partners (5 institutions)
Target: UNILAG (400 users), OAU (200), UNN (150), Covenant (150), UI (100)

University Responsibilities:
• Provide computer lab access (20+ workstations/campus)
• Designate faculty liaisons and promote via official channels
• Allow campus activations; provide institutional adoption feedback

LetsWatchOut Responsibilities:
• Free platform access during pilot; technical support
• Train ambassadors/faculty; supply marketing materials
• Share usage analytics and impact data

Tier 2: Disability Advocacy – JONAPWD
• Recruit 50+ students with disabilities; provide accessibility testing
• Co-design inclusive features; validate WCAG compliance

Tier 3: Infrastructure Partners
MainOne/Airtel Nigeria (Seeking): Subsidized internet for remote students, campus Wi-Fi sponsorship
Paystack (Existing): Payment processing, multi-currency support

Tier 4: Community Partners
20+ Student Clubs: Host weekly sessions, drive peer network growth
